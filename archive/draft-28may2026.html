Nº 01

The lede

A short orientation — who he is, what he builds, and the throughline that connects twenty years of engineering to today's agentic systems.

Natarajan Selvaraj is a Principal Software Engineer and AI Architect at Autodesk, where he leads the architecture of Lumi — an enterprise employee assistant platform built on eleven specialized MCP agents running on AWS Bedrock AgentCore. The system serves Autodesk's global workforce of more than fifteen thousand employees with conversational access to HR, IT, procurement, and knowledge workflows.

Across two decades at Autodesk, Ford, General Motors, and Ansira (CDK / Sincro), Natarajan has consistently been the engineer who turns ambiguous AI or platform initiatives into running production systems — and then the architectural patterns that make those systems maintainable for the teams that inherit them.

The hard part of enterprise AI isn't the model — it's the dual-OAuth boundary, the observability that survives a production incident, and the evaluation framework that lets you measure quality across suppliers. — On building agentic systems at scale

His current work centers on the meeting point of Generative AI, multi-agent orchestration, and enterprise-grade observability — the places where research demos become real software. He writes about what he learns on Medium, in whitepapers, and through the patterns he shares with the broader AI engineering community.
