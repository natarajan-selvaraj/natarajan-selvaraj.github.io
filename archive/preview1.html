01 — About

Profile

A short orientation — who he is, what he builds, and the throughline that connects two decades of engineering to today's agentic systems.

Natarajan Selvaraj is a Principal Engineer at Autodesk, where he leads the architecture of Lumi — an enterprise employee assistant platform built on eleven specialized MCP agents running on AWS Bedrock AgentCore. The system serves Autodesk's global workforce of more than fifteen thousand employees with conversational access to HR, IT, procurement, and knowledge workflows.

Across twenty-three years at Autodesk, Ford, General Motors, and Ansira (CDK / Sincro), he has consistently been the engineer who turns ambiguous AI or platform initiatives into running production systems — and then the architectural patterns that make those systems maintainable for the teams that inherit them.

His current work centers on the meeting point of Generative AI, multi-agent orchestration, and enterprise-grade observability — the places where research demos become real software. He writes about what he learns on Medium and through patterns shared with the broader AI engineering community.
