01 · About

Builder of reliable AI systems.

Focused on the pragmatic layer where LLMs, tools, identity, observability, and enterprise workflows have to work together.

Natarajan Selvaraj is a Principal Engineer at Autodesk, where he leads the architecture of Lumi — an enterprise employee assistant platform built on eleven specialized MCP agents running on AWS Bedrock AgentCore.

Across twenty-three years at Autodesk, Ford, General Motors, and Ansira, he has repeatedly turned ambiguous AI and platform initiatives into running production systems, then into architectural patterns that other teams can maintain.

His current work centers on Generative AI, multi-agent orchestration, RAG quality, and enterprise observability — the layer where research demos become durable software.
